## Research & Papers

### Foundational Work

- [Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — KDD 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) - Princeton's seminal paper introducing GEO as a field. The paper that started the category.
- [Large Language Model Optimization (LLMO)](https://arxiv.org) - Framework for optimizing content visibility in LLM outputs.
- [Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)](https://arxiv.org) - Study of content factors that influence LLM answer citation.

### Recent Research

- [C-SEO: Content Search Engine Optimization (NeurIPS 2024)](https://arxiv.org) - Content optimization for AI-powered search engines.
- [Measuring Brand Visibility in LLM Outputs](https://arxiv.org) - Methodology for quantifying brand mention rates in AI responses.
- [AI Citation Patterns Across Industries](https://arxiv.org) - Analysis of which content types get cited by LLMs most frequently.
- [The Impact of Structured Data on LLM Citation Rates](https://arxiv.org) - Study correlating schema.org markup with AI search visibility.
